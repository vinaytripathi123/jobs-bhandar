Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to JobsBhandar.

JobsBhandar.in is a job-discovery platform designed to help users find government jobs, private jobs, internships, work from home opportunities and other career opportunities in one place.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, protected and handled when you visit or use JobsBhandar.in.

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information handled through the JobsBhandar website and its related communication channels.

2. Information We Collect

JobsBhandar does not require users to create an account or profile merely to browse or search job listings.

Users are generally not required to provide a resume, phone number or other personal information simply to view job opportunities.

If you voluntarily contact JobsBhandar, the information you provide may include your name, email address, message or other information that you choose to share.

3. How We Use Information

Information voluntarily provided by users may be used to respond to enquiries, questions, corrections, feedback or other communications.

Information may also be used to maintain and improve the website, address technical issues, protect website security and improve the user experience.

4. Job Applications and External Websites

JobsBhandar may provide links to employer, government, recruitment or other external websites where users can apply for job opportunities.

When you leave JobsBhandar and submit information on an external website, that information is handled according to the privacy policy and terms of that external website.

JobsBhandar does not control the information practices of external websites.

5. Third-Party Websites and Links

Job listings may contain links to websites operated by employers, government organisations, recruitment platforms or other third parties.

Users should review the privacy policy and terms of any external website before providing personal information or submitting an application.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

JobsBhandar or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies where required for website functionality, security, analytics or improving the user experience.

If the use of such technologies changes materially, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

7. Job Information and Accuracy

JobsBhandar aims to provide useful and relevant job information. However, vacancies, eligibility requirements, salaries, deadlines, locations and recruitment processes may change.

Users should verify important information from the relevant employer, government department, recruitment authority or official source before applying.

8. No Employment Guarantee

JobsBhandar is a job-information and discovery platform. Listing or displaying a job opportunity does not guarantee employment, selection, interview calls, salary, internship placement or recruitment results.

9. Fraud and Payment Safety

JobsBhandar does not authorise anyone to demand money from job seekers in exchange for employment through JobsBhandar.

Users should be cautious of anyone asking for money, passwords, OTPs, banking credentials or other sensitive information in exchange for a job opportunity.

10. Data Security

JobsBhandar takes reasonable measures to protect information handled through its website and communication channels.

However, no internet transmission, website or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

Information voluntarily provided to JobsBhandar may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to communications, maintain records, address disputes, improve services or comply with applicable legal requirements.

12. Privacy Choices

Users may choose not to provide personal information when it is not required for the particular interaction. Users may also contact JobsBhandar regarding questions or concerns about information they have voluntarily provided.

13. Children's Privacy

JobsBhandar is intended for general job seekers and career-related users. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

14. Intellectual Property and Third-Party Rights

JobsBhandar respects the intellectual property and rights of third parties. Information, trademarks, logos, job descriptions or other materials belonging to employers or third parties remain subject to their respective rights.

15. Website Availability

JobsBhandar aims to keep the website available and functional, but temporary interruptions may occur due to maintenance, technical issues, hosting, network problems or circumstances beyond our control.

16. Legal Compliance

JobsBhandar may process or disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, legal processes, lawful requests or to protect the rights, safety and security of the website and its users.
